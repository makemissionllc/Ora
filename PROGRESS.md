# Progress

Last updated: 2026-11-17, at commit `b5e1949`.

## Done
- Site permissions (`d47a696`): when a site asks for location, camera, microphone or notifications, a card offers Allow, Not now or Block. Allow and Block are saved per site and per profile in `ora_site_permissions`; Not now saves nothing. Private tabs save nothing and show "Private tab: your choice is not remembered." Settings > Privacy > Site permissions shows the count of saved sites ("Saved for N sites. Tap to clear." or "No saved choices yet") and clears them after a confirmation. Android's own permission is requested the first time a site asks. Verified on the tablet: a private-tab card; a private request saved nothing; camera Not now, Block (and the saved Block answer on the next ask); location, microphone and notifications Allow; Settings count "Saved for 1 site", the clear confirmation, and the cleared row with "No saved choices yet". Verified on the phone (OnePlus CPH2745): location Not now (nothing saved), Allow (saved, Android location granted), Block (saved false, page reports the denial); camera Not now (nothing saved) and Allow (saved true, log "User allowed camera"); microphone Allow (saved true, Android RECORD_AUDIO granted); notifications Allow (saved true). Not checked: the phone's Settings clear flow; a private tab on the phone; camera and microphone Block on the phone; whether a notification is shown after Allow; the card placement under the status bar (see Known issues).
- Browsing history (`b5e1949`): a History segment in the tablet sidebar and the phone Library lists the pages you opened, newest first, grouped by day. Search by title or address, remove one entry with its ×, or clear all after a confirmation. Each profile keeps its own history. Saving can be turned off in Settings > Privacy, and private tabs are never saved. Verified on tablet (Anime profile): 3 visits listed under Today; the × removed the google.com row (log "Removed one history entry"); search "animex" showed only animex.one; Clear history: Cancel kept both rows, Clear emptied the list to "No history yet" (log "Cleared 2 history entries"); with saving off the "Saving is off" note appeared, and on again it went (logs "Save browsing history off" and "on"). Not checked: the phone (not connected over ADB); that a visit is not recorded while saving is off; private tabs; the Personal profile's history; the 1000-entry cap.
- Reopen closed tab in the ⋯ menu (`9ef0c1d`): the last 10 closed tabs (not private tabs or start pages) are kept with their back and forward pages. The row under Find in page shows the title of the tab it would reopen. With nothing closed it says "Nothing closed yet", and tapping it shows "No closed tabs to reopen". Verified on tablet: the empty-state snackbar; closing iana.org from the tab overview; the row then showed its title; tapping it reopened iana.org with back enabled; logs "Closed tab 0", "Reopened closed tab 0" and "Restored history". Not checked: the phone (not tapped, see the verification log); closing and reopening more than one tab; the 10-tab limit.

## Pending

### Motion
- Sidebar and bars opening from their origin (a scale from the hamburger, as on iOS). Only the fade and width expansion exist; the origin scale is not built.
- Layout changes (bar style, Compact shrink) and tab switches still cut over without a shared transition.
- The History "Clear history" confirmation is a standard dialog. It does not morph from its button yet (AGENTS.md rule 10).

### Tab management (from the Chrome and Safari comparison)

- Drag one tab onto another to make a group, and drag tabs into or out of groups.
- Name and colour-code tab groups.
- Mini tab switcher at the bottom for quick switching within a group.
- Inactive tabs: move unused tabs to a separate collection after a chosen time.
- Recent tabs: list tabs open on other devices signed into the same account. Needs a sync design first.
- Search bookmarks and history from one search field. Open-tab and history search are done (see Done); a single field across bookmarks and history is still open.

### Better app usage

- Profiles: cookies and logins are still shared between profiles. Bookmarks and history are kept per profile. Keeping cookies and logins apart needs a storage decision.
- Built-in ad blocking beyond tracking protection: Privacy already has "Block ads and trackers" (GeckoView strict tracking protection). A fuller blocker would need a filter-list design, a choice of lists, and per-site exceptions before any work.

### Carried over

- Tablet check for the Secure DNS row in Settings > Privacy (see verification log).
- Reader mode. The approach is undecided. GeckoView `about:reader` has not been tested.
- Shrink-on-scroll on tablet: bars currently hide completely. The design calls for shrinking to smaller controls.
- Tablet Tabs section in Settings, not re-checked since the rail change.
- Preloading (for example, prefetching a link on long press). Needs a design, and it must not load pages the user did not ask for.
- Network: GeckoView 160 has no public DNS prefetch or preconnect control. Those options are internal and would need a decision before any work.
- Typing in the tablet address bar with the on-screen keyboard can insert spaces and capitals, so the address becomes a search. Not reproduced on 2026-11-17 (landscape, Gboard): "mozilla.org" typed letter by letter, with no inserted spaces or capitals, loaded mozilla.org on Go. An earlier search came from a keyboard that had dropped mid-typing. It recurred on 2026-11-17 on the tablet: "http://nosuchsite.invalid" was typed with a space after the dot, so the address field shows "nosuchsite. invalid". Open again.

- Site permissions: during tests a card was answered with no tap recorded (tablet about 18:21, phone about 18:24, both on the test page). Cause not found. On the phone, an older saved camera choice from test data could explain an automatic Allow. Not reproduced.
